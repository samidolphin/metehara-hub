Mataaharaa: Carraa Hin Mul’anne Ija Risaa’n Arguu! 🌱

Yeroo tokko tokko magaalaa tokko caalaatti bareechuuf baajata guddaa qofa qabaachuun hin ga’u. Waan jiru karaa addaatiin ilaaluun, carraa hin mul’anne adda baasuun, boru yaadanii har’a hojjechuu danda’uun “ija risaa” barbaada. 🦅 Mul’ata misoomaa kana hojii qabatamaa keessatti kan mul’isaa jirtu jijjiirama Magaalaa Mataaharaa keessatti mul’achaa jiru keessaa, fakkeenyi ajaa’ibaa tokko har’a isin waliin qooduu barbaannu kunooti. Daandii asfaltii bareedaan wiirtuu Magaalaa Mataaharaa irraa gara Buufata Baaburaa Itoophiyaa–Jibuutii geessu, waggoota hedduuf gidduutti bakka daraaraa, muka fi biqiltuu dhaabuuf qophaa’e qaba ture. Haa ta’u malee, carraan kun yeroo dheeraaf xiyyeeffannoo gahaa osoo hin argatin ture. Har’a garuu haalli isaa jijjiirameera. 🌿 Bakkichi dallaa ammayyaa fi hawwataa ta’een miidhagfamaa, biqiltoota naannoo gammoojjii fi qilleensa ho’aa sanaaf mijatoo ta’an dhaabuun, akkasumas qindaa’ina sirrii biqiltoota magariisaa fayyadamuun gara wiirtuu miidhagina magaalaa hawwataa ta’etti jijjiiramaa jira. 🌱✨ Sagantaan dhaabbii biqiltuu kunis Bulchiinsa Magaalaa Mataaharaa fi Future Generation Organization waliin ta’uun kan raawwatame yoo ta’u, Kantibaan Bulchiinsa Magaalaa Mataaharaa Obbo Addisuu Waqqoo fi Qindeessaan Future Generation Organization Magaalaa Mataaharaa waliin biqiltuu dhaabuun mul’ata magariisaa gara fuulduraa magaalaa kanaa hojii irra oolchanii agarsiisaniiru. 🌳 Hojiin kun biqiltuu dhaabuu qofa miti. Inni jaalala magaalaa keenyaaf qabnu, itti-gaafatamummaa biyya keenyaaf qabnu, yaada dhaloota dhufuuf qabnu, akkasumas hojii Asqama Magariisaa (Green Legacy) qabatamaan kan mul’isuudha. 🌍💚 Biqiltoonni dhaabaman akka guddatan, miidhagina magaalaa akka dabalan, kunuunsi itti fufiinsa qabu akka taasifamuufis waliin karoorfamuu isaa ibsameera. Dhaabbii biqiltuu kana eeguuf, miidhaa irraa ittisuufis dallaan bareedaan sadarkaa magaalaa eeggate, ogeessi yeroo hunda hordofu, akkasumas taankariin kuusaa bishaanii liitira 10,000 qophaa’aniiru. 💧🌱 Kantiibaan Magaalaa Mataaharaa Obbo Addisuu Waqqoo hojii kun bulchiinsa qofaatiin xumuramuu akka hin dandeenye ibsuun, jiraattonni Magaalaa Mataaharaa biqiltoota dhaabaman eeguu, kunuunsuu fi miidhagina magaalaa isaanii waliin ta’uun tiksuu keessatti gahee fi itti-gaafatamummaa isaanii akka ba’an waamicha dhiyeessaniiru. 🌱 Biqiltuun har’a dhaabnu, boru miidhagina magaalaa keenyaati. Misooma magariisaa har’a kunuunsinu, boru dhaala dhalootaati. 💚 Mataaharaan jijjiiramaa jirti — nutis jijjiirama kana keessatti hirmaattota haa taanu!

🌿 Green Development & Beautification

Matahara: Seeing Untapped Potential Through an Eagle’s Eye 🌱

Sometimes, transforming and beautifying a town requires more than a large budget. It requires the ability to see existing spaces from a different perspective, identify opportunities that others may overlook, envision the future, and take meaningful action today. It requires an “eagle’s eye.” 🦅 One remarkable example of this development vision can be seen in the ongoing transformation of Matahara City—and this is one of the stories we are proud to share today. For years, the beautiful asphalt road connecting the heart of Matahara town to the Ethio-Djibouti railway station had a designated central space for flowers, trees, and seedlings. Yet, despite its potential, this space remained largely overlooked for a long time. Today, that is changing. 🌿 Through thoughtful urban design, the area is being transformed with a modern and attractive fencing system, carefully selected plants suited to the city’s arid environment, and a well-planned green landscape. What was once an underutilized space is gradually becoming an attractive feature of Matahara’s urban environment—a place that can enhance the city’s beauty and provide a more pleasant setting for residents and visitors. This impressive tree-planting and urban beautification initiative is being implemented through collaboration between the Matahara City Administration and Future Generation Organization. H.E. Ato Addisu Wako, Mayor of Matahara town, and the Matahara City Coordinator of Future Generation Organization jointly participated in the tree-planting activity, demonstrating in action their shared vision for a greener and more beautiful future for the town. 🌳 This initiative is about much more than planting seedlings. It represents a commitment to the city, a responsibility to the country, a vision for future generations, and a practical expression of Ethiopia’s Green Legacy. 🌍💚 Importantly, the initiative also includes plans for continued care and maintenance to ensure that the newly planted seedlings grow successfully and contribute to the city’s long-term beauty. To protect the plantation and support its sustainability, a modern city-standard protective fence has been prepared, along with continuous professional supervision and a 10,000-liter water storage tanker to support regular watering and maintenance. 💧🌱 Mayor Addisu Wako has also emphasized that this work cannot be completed by the administration alone. He called upon the residents of Matahara to take shared responsibility by protecting and caring for the newly planted seedlings and by collectively safeguarding the beauty of their city. The seedlings we plant today will become the beauty of our city tomorrow. The green development we nurture today will become a legacy for future generations. Matahara is changing—and let us all become active participants in that transformation!
